wheeler2016 verified GEODESIC.md, Direct smooth-geodesic distances The geodesic equation and an elementary angular primitive for this paraboloid are given by Wheeler (2016), pp. 5–6, Eqs. Author's notes hosted at Reed College. Full source PDF Page 1 identifies author, title and date. Page 5 gives the metric and K=4/(1+4u)² with u=rho², the angular ODE (9.3), and primitive (9.4). Page 6 gives its normalized form and joins the inward and outward branches at the turning point. Codex read the full-source passages on 2026-09-05. Its length primitive, shortest-path branch proof, validation, and MDS asymptotics are derived independently in GEODESIC.md and are not attributed to the source.
