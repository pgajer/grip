# GKK LGKK Paper Outline

Date: 2026-04-05

## Recommendation

This is the right time to revisit Paper 2.

I would keep the core paper identity, but I would tighten the framing:

1. Keep `GKK` as the mathematical center of the paper.
2. Keep `LGKK` as the practical sparse approximation.
3. Promote `MISF-GKK` from a secondary integration idea to a real third method-family contribution.
4. Demote weighted `GRIP` from the main scaling story to a secondary comparison/application section.
5. Treat tie handling as important implementation detail, but not necessarily as a headline contribution unless the dedicated evidence is strong.

So the paper should become a `GKK + LGKK + MISF-GKK` paper, while still staying evidence-driven and disciplined about what belongs in the main narrative.

## Paper Identity

### Main question

How should a `KK`-style objective be modified when the realized geometric discrepancy should be measured along the embedded graph rather than by straight-line chord distance?

### Main answer

Replace the chord term by embedded path length to obtain a full geodesic `KK` objective, approximate it sparsely with landmark-linked pairs, and use the same objective family inside a MISF-based multiscale optimizer for larger graphs.

### What the paper should claim

- `GKK` is a coherent path-based analogue of classical `KK`.
- `LGKK` is the practical sparse approximation to `GKK`.
- On weighted geometric graphs, geodesic polishing improves geodesic fidelity substantially over plain `KK`.
- `LGKK` captures most of the benefit of full `GKK` at much lower runtime.
- `MISF-GKK` is a coherent multiscale extension of the same geodesic objective family, not just an implementation trick inside weighted `GRIP`.

### What the paper should not claim

- weighted `GRIP` alone is the main scientific result
- the paper is primarily about package architecture
- weighted `GRIP` is the primary multiscale contribution
- broad scalability beyond the evidence we actually run

## Evidence Status At A Glance

### Already strong

- Full `GKK` and sparse `LGKK` are implemented in `R/grip_quality.R`.
- A full public `MISF-GKK` family now exists in `R/grip_geodesic_misf_kk.R`:
  - `grip.prepare.misf.geodesic.kk()`
  - `grip.optimize.misf.geodesic.kk()`
  - `grip.score.misf.geodesic.kk()`
- Public man pages exist for all three `MISF-GKK` entry points.
- The mesh benchmark is already meaningful:
  - 3 surface families
  - 3 sizes
  - 2 embedding dimensions
  - 3 seeds
  - 54 benchmark cases from shared starts
- Current mesh aggregate result:
  - in 3D, mean full-`GKK` relative RMSE drops from about `0.0254` for `KK` to about `0.0067` for `KK -> GKK`
  - `KK -> LGKK` reaches about `0.0069`
  - `LGKK` is roughly seven times faster than full `GKK`
- Core implementation validation is already in good shape:
  - targeted current test run passed `212` checks across `geodesic-kk`, `landmark-geodesic-kk`, `layout-globalrep`, `layout-trace`, `layout-weighted-globalrep`, and `layout-weighted-trace`
  - the dedicated `MISF-GKK` scaffold tests also pass (`32` checks in `test-geodesic-misf-kk-scaffold.R`)

### Encouraging but not yet sufficient

- The cross-family weighted smoke panel already exists and is directionally supportive.
- In 3D across five smoke-panel families:
  - `KK` mean full-`GKK` relative RMSE is about `0.0448`
  - `KK -> GKK` is about `0.0280`
  - `KK -> LGKK` is about `0.0228`
- That is good evidence for direction, but it is still smoke-level evidence rather than a final paper benchmark.

### Currently conditional

- Weighted `GRIP` should remain conditional and secondary.
- If the weighted-`GRIP` evidence stays modest or noisy, it should move to an appendix or discussion/future-work subsection without harming the core paper.

## Section-Level Claim Discipline

### Non-negotiable main-text claims

- `C1`: chord distance is the wrong realized-distance surrogate in the target setting
- `C2`: full `GKK` is the clean objective-level fix
- `C3`: `LGKK` recovers most of the quality gain more cheaply
- `C4`: `MISF-GKK` is a coherent multiscale optimizer built from the same objective family

### Conditional claim

- `C5`: weighted `GRIP` remains a useful comparison or application route once `MISF-GKK` exists as the cleaner multiscale geodesic family

If `C5` is not strongly supported by `EXP-8`, it should not sit in the abstract as a coequal contribution.

## What I Would Change Relative To The 2026-04-03 Outline

1. I would make the evidence plan first-class rather than implicit.
2. I would promote `MISF-GKK` from a side section to a real contribution.
3. I would demote weighted `GRIP` from the central scaling story.
4. I would avoid promising too much on tie handling unless the dedicated example is strong.
5. I would explicitly separate:
   - what is already supported
   - what still needs to be run
   - what could be dropped without harming the paper
